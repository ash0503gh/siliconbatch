## Technical Breakthrough & Overview

Rural and remote communities worldwide suffer from disjointed supply chains: transporting heavy cargo, essential medical supplies, and critical infrastructure parts across island archipelagos, mountainous regions, and roadless terrain often requires slow diesel ferries or prohibitively expensive piloted turboprop airplanes. Pyka solved this logistical divide by developing the **Pelican Cargo**: the world's largest commercially certified zero-emission autonomous cargo aircraft.

Engineered specifically for heavy uncrewed logistics, Pelican Cargo can lift up to 500 kilograms (1,100 pounds) of freight, take off and land on rudimentary 150-meter unpaved grass or gravel airstrips, and navigate complex terrain autonomously without a human pilot onboard. By combining electric powertrain simplicity with proprietary triple-redundant flight control software, Pelican Cargo reduces operational transport costs per ton-mile by over 60% compared to fossil-fuel planes.

## Hardware & Neural Architecture

Pyka engineered Pelican Cargo with safety-critical aerospace rigor and edge-native autonomy:
- **Redundant Electric Propulsion**: Four electric motor drive units producing a combined 100 kW of power, driven by an aerospace-certified 50 kWh swappable lithium-ion battery pack that recharges or swaps in under 10 minutes.
- **Triple-Modular Redundant Flight Computer**: The avionics architecture features three independent flight computers executing lock-step voter algorithms to detect and isolate hardware faults in mid-flight without service interruption.
- **Forward-Facing 3D LiDAR & Optical SLAM**: Scans terrain, trees, power lines, and runway hazards in real time during approach and landing phases, generating high-frequency digital surface elevation maps to execute precision landings in zero-visibility conditions.
- **Dynamic Contingency Engine**: Autonomous algorithms continuously calculate viable glide cones and emergency landing waypoints, guaranteeing safe diversion even in the unlikely event of complete propulsion loss.

## Real-World Benchmarks & Impact

Pelican Cargo is operating commercially with global air cargo carriers and defense logistics networks:
- **FAA & International Certification**: Earned regulatory operational approvals across North America and Europe, completing commercial customer delivery flights over remote island chains.
- **Short-Field Takeoff Performance**: Operates safely from austere airstrips as short as 150 meters (500 feet), unlocking direct freight access to thousands of communities lacking paved runways.
- **Carbon Elimination**: Eliminates 100% of direct tailpipe carbon and particulate emissions, saving tens of thousands of metric tons of CO2 annually per deployed airframe fleet.

## Open-Source, Access & Future Roadmap

Pyka delivers Pelican Cargo through turnkey aircraft sales and fleet operational agreements:
- **Commercial Fleet Deployments**: Partnering with regional cargo carriers, humanitarian aid organizations, and postal authorities to establish zero-emission air freight corridors.
- **Airspace Integration**: Collaborating with civil aviation regulators on autonomous Detect-and-Avoid (DAA) standards for beyond visual line of sight (BVLOS) operations in shared civilian airspace.
- **Hybrid Extended-Range Variant**: Pyka's roadmap includes a hybrid-electric range extension module targeting 1,000+ nautical mile missions for trans-oceanic humanitarian freight routes.
