## Problem
Fortune 500 enterprises face enormous roadblocks attempting to deploy generative AI directly into mission-critical business workflows. Off-the-shelf foundation models lack context regarding proprietary data silos, regulatory compliance guardrails, and deterministic ERP/CRM interfaces. Standard prototypes frequently hallucinate, expose sensitive internal corporate intellectual property, or fail compliance scrutiny in regulated sectors like financial services and life sciences.

## Solution & Innovation
Distyl AI operates as an elite enterprise deployment platform in direct technical partnership with OpenAI. The platform embeds customized generative AI agents and reasoning pipelines directly inside enterprise firewalls, orchestrating complex workflows across supply chain logistics, internal knowledge management, and financial forecasting. Distyl pairs state-of-the-art frontier model weights with verifiable enterprise knowledge graphs to ensure zero hallucinations and deterministic operational outcomes.

## Technology & Architecture
Distyl's enterprise architecture is engineered for rigorous security, compliance, and multi-tenant isolation:
- **Zero-Data-Retention Model Gateways**: Direct private integrations with OpenAI infrastructure ensuring customer inputs are never used for base model training.
- **Dynamic Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**: Employs semantic vector routing coupled with hybrid BM25 and knowledge-graph traversal across legacy enterprise databases.
- **Deterministic Orchestration**: Built upon custom Ray clusters and asynchronous microservices, validating intermediate LLM outputs through symbolic logic checkers before committing state changes.

## Team & YC Journey
Co-founders Arjun Prakash and Derek Ho bring extensive leadership backgrounds from Palantir Technologies, where they spearheaded large-scale data engineering deployments across defense, government, and commercial sectors. Selected for Y Combinator's W24 batch, Distyl raised $27M in venture backing led by Lightspeed Venture Partners with strategic participation from OpenAI Startup Fund and prominent tech luminaries.
