## Problem
Agricultural crop protection and regional freight cargo transport rely on aging, hazardous, carbon-intensive fossil fuel aircraft. Human-piloted crop dusters suffer from one of the highest fatality rates in commercial aviation due to low-altitude maneuvers and chemical exposure. Furthermore, conventional regional logistics networks are economically unviable for short-haul island and remote freight delivery due to high pilot costs, fuel volatility, and runway length constraints.

## Solution & Innovation
Pyka manufactures certified autonomous electric airplanes engineered specifically for commercial scale operations. Their flagship agricultural platform, **Pelican Spray**, operates fully autonomously inches above complex canopy terrain at night and during inclement weather, dramatically improving yield and operator safety. Their heavy-lift cargo variant, **Pelican Cargo**, is the world's largest zero-emission autonomous cargo aircraft, carrying up to 400 lbs of payload over 200 miles from unpaved, austere airstrips.

## Technology & Architecture
Pyka's vertically integrated aircraft architecture pairs aerospace rigor with physical AI autonomy:
- **Redundant Fly-By-Wire Avionics**: Triple-redundant flight computer architectures running deterministic C++ real-time operating systems with certified fail-operational logic.
- **Terrain Following & Collision Avoidance**: Real-time forward-looking LiDAR, stereo computer vision, and high-resolution digital elevation models for dynamic contour tracking at high airspeeds.
- **Modular Swappable Battery Architecture**: Liquid-cooled lithium-ion battery packs that can be hot-swapped in under five minutes to maintain maximum fleet operational tempo.

## Team & YC Journey
Pyka was founded by Michael Norcia, Chuma Kabaghe, and Kyle Moore—aerospace engineers with roots at Zee Aero, Joby Aviation, and Cora. Supported by Y Combinator, Prime Movers Lab, and prominent deep-tech venture firms, Pyka has logged tens of thousands of commercial autonomous flight hours across North America, Latin America, and Oceania, pioneering the regulatory pathway for heavy commercial autonomous aviation.
