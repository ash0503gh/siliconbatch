## Problem
Traditional customer support automation relies on brittle decision-tree chatbots that frustrate end users and handle only basic FAQs. When a user requests an order modification, billing refund, flight cancellation, or identity verification, standard bots instantly fail and route the ticket to human representatives. As global support ticket volumes skyrocket, enterprise support teams suffer from high churn, soaring labor costs, and slow response times.

## Solution & Innovation
Decagon creates human-grade conversational AI agents that operate with true autonomy across enterprise customer support. Rather than acting as mere knowledge retrieval bots, Decagon agents execute multi-step business transactions directly within internal billing systems, ticketing CRMs (like Zendesk and Salesforce), and inventory databases. Decagon routinely resolves upwards of 60% of complex customer inquiries end-to-end without human intervention, maintaining complete conversational nuance and empathetic brand voice.

## Technology & Architecture
Decagon's enterprise orchestration framework is built for real-time safety, auditability, and deep workflow integration:
- **Autonomous Tool-Use Engine**: Powered by fine-tuned multi-agent foundation architectures trained on hundreds of thousands of successful support resolutions.
- **Workflow State Management via Temporal**: Guarantees deterministic execution of side-effect actions (such as initiating refunds or executing database updates) with rollback capabilities.
- **Enterprise Guardrail Layer**: Real-time evaluation layers monitor sentiment, hallucination indicators, and policy boundaries before any message is transmitted to the customer.

## Team & YC Journey
Co-founders Jesse Zhang (Harvard alum and founder of Lowkey, acquired by Niantic) and Ashwin Sreenivas (Cambridge graduate and founder of Helia, acquired by Dataminr) launched Decagon to transform enterprise service operations. Emerging from Y Combinator's W24 batch, Decagon raised $35M in Series A funding led by Accel and Andreessen Horowitz, serving high-volume consumer and B2B leaders including Eventbrite, Substack, and Bilt Rewards.
