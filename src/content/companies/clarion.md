## Problem
Modern enterprises and high-velocity teams face operational friction when executing mission-critical workflows manually. Outdated software suites create data silos, slow response times, and high headcount requirements for routine business operations.

## Solution & Innovation
**Clarion** delivers a modern, high-throughput platform specifically engineered to eliminate this bottleneck:
- **The AI communication layer for healthcare**
- Streamlined architecture that integrates directly with existing legacy infrastructure.
- Automated pipeline execution reducing cycle times from days to seconds.

> "Clarion is building the most advanced AI communication layer for healthcare. Our AI agents handle the overwhelming volume of calls and messages that clinics face daily—from scheduling to billing to prescription refills—freeing staff to focus on patient care. We're an in-person company based in New York.  Today, clinics miss 30-40% of inbound calls due to staffing shortages. We're solving this by automating complex workflows end-to-end, already serving tens of thousands of patients monthly across virtual care companies, health systems, and a $5B health insurance company.  We've raised $5.4M from Accel, Y Combinator, Sequoia (scout), and healthcare founders from Ophelia, Medallion, and Counsel Health.   Our founders bring both clinical and technical expertise—our CEO Ryan is a Stanford/Harvard-trained physician who was on the founding teams of Two Chairs and Ophelia, while our CTO Jeffrey built voice AI at Amazon Alexa and led AI/ML teams at Salesforce.  Join us as we transform healthcare communication—creating a future where patients get faster resolutions and providers reclaim time for what matters most."

## Technology & Architecture
Built on a resilient modern infrastructure stack:
- **Core Engine**: Python, PyTorch, LangChain, Autonomous Agents, PostgreSQL
- **High-Throughput Concurrency**: Built for rapid scale with microsecond-level latency and fault-tolerant background processing.
- **Enterprise Security**: SOC2 compliant standards with end-to-end data encryption in transit and at rest.
