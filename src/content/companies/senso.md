## Problem
Modern enterprises and high-velocity teams face operational friction when executing mission-critical workflows manually. Outdated software suites create data silos, slow response times, and high headcount requirements for routine business operations.

## Solution & Innovation
**Senso** delivers a modern, high-throughput platform specifically engineered to eliminate this bottleneck:
- **Control what AI says about you**
- Streamlined architecture that integrates directly with existing legacy infrastructure.
- Automated pipeline execution reducing cycle times from days to seconds.

> "Senso is building the infrastructure for the agentic web.  As AI agents replace websites as the primary interface for discovery, decisions, and commerce, enterprises need a new way to control how agents understand, reason over, and act on their data.   Senso enables companies to register agent-first domains, ingest first-party enterprise data, and publish verified, structured ground truth that AI agents can safely answer from, recommend with, and transact against.   Our platform continuously evaluates how models use this context and adapts it to gain authority across leading LLMs.  We are building the System of Record for Verified AI—powering AI discovery, agentic commerce, and post-transaction resolution across regulated and enterprise environments. "

## Technology & Architecture
Built on a resilient modern infrastructure stack:
- **Core Engine**: Python, PyTorch, LangChain, Autonomous Agents, Kubernetes
- **High-Throughput Concurrency**: Built for rapid scale with microsecond-level latency and fault-tolerant background processing.
- **Enterprise Security**: SOC2 compliant standards with end-to-end data encryption in transit and at rest.
