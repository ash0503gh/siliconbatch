## Overview

Founded by Sorbonne University, ENS, ParisTech, and INRIA, **Agoranov** is the most prolific deep tech incubator in France. Its alumni have generated billions in enterprise value and created breakthrough technologies ranging from **Prophesee's neuromorphic event-based vision sensors** to **Wandercraft's self-balancing robotics exoskeletons**.

Operating from central Paris, Agoranov specializes in helping complex, IP-dense hardware, electronics, and semiconductor spinouts commercialize without giving away equity.

---

## Investment Structure

- **Equity Taken**: **0.0%**. Agoranov is a non-profit public incubator and takes zero equity or warrants.
- **Funding Leverage**: Accepted startups unlock immediate access to the French **Bpifrance Deeptech Plan**, unlocking between **€150,000 to €2,000,000** in non-dilutive grants, subsidies, and zero-interest innovation loans.
- **Deep Tech Laboratory Access**: Startups receive access to premier academic micro-nanofabrication facilities, optics benches, and cleanrooms throughout the Paris-Saclay academic cluster.
