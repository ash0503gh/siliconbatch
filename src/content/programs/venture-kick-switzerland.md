## Overview

**Venture Kick** is the premier pre-seed initiative for Swiss deep tech startups. Run in close collaboration with **ETH Zurich** and **EPFL**, the program has funded over 1,000 spinouts that have gone on to raise billions in follow-on venture rounds and achieve global hardware leadership.

For teams building advanced sensors, custom silicon architectures, medical robotics, or precision mechatronics, Venture Kick offers rigorous milestone-based capital and cleanroom access.

---

## The 3-Stage Kick Process

- **Stage 1**: **CHF 10,000** grant for initial validation.
- **Stage 2**: **CHF 40,000** for prototype benchmark testing.
- **Stage 3**: **CHF 100,000** convertible seed check for commercial manufacturing setup.
- **Total Funding**: **CHF 150,000** (~$175,000 USD) designed to propel teams directly into a multi-million-dollar Seed round.
- **Cleanroom & Labs**: Access to world-class micro-engineering facilities including ETH Zurich FIRST and EPFL CMi.
