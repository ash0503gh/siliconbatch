## Overview

**HAX** is SOSV's flagship program for hard tech, mechatronics, industrial robotics, and deep electronics. Operating out of a massive 35,000 sq ft facility in Newark, NJ, HAX provides not just capital, but a small army of full-time electrical, mechanical, and industrial design engineers who work shoulder-to-shoulder with your team.

For startups whose biggest risk is **Design for Manufacturing (DFM)** and supply chain procurement, HAX is unmatched in bridging the prototype-to-production gap.

---

## Terms and Investment Breakdown

- **Total Investment**: **$250,000** upfront investment for **~8% equity** (combination of common/preferred terms).
- **Follow-On Reserve**: SOSV frequently participates with up to \$1M+ in Series A/B rounds for breakout graduates.
- **Engineering Staff Support**: Access to in-house CAD specialists, firmware engineers, and regulatory compliance consultants at zero billable hourly rate.
- **IP Retention**: You maintain complete, unabridged ownership of all patents and designs.
