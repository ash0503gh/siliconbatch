## Overview

**Blueprint** is Founders, Inc.’s flagship 12-week accelerator designed exclusively for frontier engineers moving from pure software into **atoms, mechanics, and physical AI systems**. Hosted at the historic Fort Mason pier in San Francisco, the program combines heavy equipment, institutional capital, and community living.

Unlike traditional software incubators where founders sit behind MacBooks, Blueprint is built around a fully functional **hardware workshop and micro-fab lab** overlooking the Golden Gate Bridge.

---

## The Deal & Legal Terms

Founders, Inc. deploys a standard, founder-friendly instrument:
- **Capital**: **$150,000** upfront investment on a post-money SAFE.
- **Equity**: **7% ownership stake** on standard YC-style terms.
- **Valuation Cap**: **$3.5M Post-Money Cap**, providing strong upside for early teams.
- **IP Protection**: **100% IP retention** by the founders. No claims, no royalties, no right of first refusal on proprietary hardware patents.
- **Living Subsidy**: Subsidized founder housing in San Francisco Marina district + \$10,000 discretionary stipend to cover materials and living expenses.

---

## Prototyping Facilities & Lab Access

Blueprint founders receive 24/7 access to the Founders, Inc. hardware shop:
1. **Electronics Fab**: Manual and automated Pick-and-Place machine, reflow ovens, solder fume extractors, Rigol digital storage oscilloscopes, and thermal imaging cameras.
2. **Machining & Enclosures**: High-precision CNC milling units, laser cutters for acrylic/wood enclosures, and filament + resin 3D printing farms.
3. **Compute Node**: Low-latency local connection to an internal multi-GPU rig for robotics imitation learning and vision-language-action (VLA) inference.

---

## Application & Selection Process

- **Rolling Interviews**: While the hard deadline is **October 18, 2026**, applications are reviewed weekly.
- **Hardware Walkthrough**: Shortlisted teams are asked to conduct a 15-minute video demonstration of physical rigs, schematics, or simulation logs.
- **Batch Size**: Strictly capped at 10-12 companies per cohort to maximize hands-on shop access and partner engagement.
