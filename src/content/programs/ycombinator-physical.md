## Overview

Y Combinator is the world's most successful startup accelerator. In recent cohorts, YC has aggressively expanded its focus on **Physical AI, humanoid robotics, custom silicon, and defense hardware**.

YC's network gives hardware founders unprecedented leverage: instant access to suppliers, top software engineering talent, and a Demo Day audience capable of funding tens of millions in hardware development.

---

## The Standard Deal

YC invests **$500,000** in every company on standard terms:
1. **$125,000** on a post-money SAFE in return for **7% equity**.
2. **$375,000** on an **uncapped SAFE** with a Most Favored Nation (MFN) provision, protecting your valuation when raising larger seed rounds.
