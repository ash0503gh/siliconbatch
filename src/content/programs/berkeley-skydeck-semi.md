## Overview

**Berkeley SkyDeck's Chip & Hardware Track** is dedicated to early-stage founders architecting custom ASICs, RISC-V extensions, photonic chips, and advanced sensory electronics. Operating with close ties to the world-renowned UC Berkeley College of Engineering, this track provides access to multimillion-dollar EDA licenses and physical cleanroom facilities that are typically impossible for pre-seed teams to afford.

---

## Investment Terms & University Association

- **Direct Check**: **$200,000** from the Berkeley SkyDeck Fund via a founder-friendly post-money SAFE.
- **Equity**: **7.5% equity**.
- **EDA Licenses**: Subsidized commercial software tooling agreements covering Cadence, Synopsys, and Siemens EDA suites.
- **Non-UC Founders Welcome**: While university spinouts are common, teams with no prior Berkeley affiliation are accepted and welcomed.
