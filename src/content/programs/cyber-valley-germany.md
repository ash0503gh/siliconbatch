## Overview

**Cyber Valley** is Europe’s largest research consortium in artificial intelligence, computer vision, and robotics. Backed by the state of Baden-Württemberg, the **Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems**, the University of Stuttgart, the University of Tübingen, and industrial giants including **Bosch, Mercedes-Benz, and Porsche**, this program supports physical AI founders solving complex manipulation, perception, and kinematic challenges.

---

## The Non-Dilutive European Advantage

- **Equity Taken**: **0.0%**. Cyber Valley takes zero equity in your company.
- **Grant Package**: Access to **€200,000** in non-dilutive founder living stipends and prototyping vouchers through German state innovation initiatives (EXIST, Cyber Valley Venture Fund).
- **Robotics Test Arena**: Deploy your algorithms directly onto research-grade Franka Emika and KUKA industrial arms, with precision force-torque feedback and multi-camera optical tracking rigs.
