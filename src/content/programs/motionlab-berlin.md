## Overview

**MotionLab.Berlin** operates Europe’s most comprehensive hardware and deep tech prototyping campus. Located in Berlin-Treptow across 4,500 square meters, MotionLab provides early-stage physical tech founders with every machine required to progress from CAD sketches to functional pre-series production runs.

Their flagship accelerator combines capital, workshop machinery, and direct supplier access to help robotics and advanced electronics startups bridge the European hardware funding gap.

---

## Terms and Investment Framework

- **Investment**: **€150,000** provided via a founder-friendly convertible instrument.
- **Valuation Cap**: **€2.15M cap**, tailored for European pre-seed rounds.
- **Zero Billable Machine Rates**: Full, unbilled access to CNC milling, waterjet cutting, 3D printing farms, and SMD electronics soldering stations for the entire 16 weeks.
- **100% IP Retention**: Founders retain 100% ownership over all schematics, mechanical drawings, firmware code, and patents.
