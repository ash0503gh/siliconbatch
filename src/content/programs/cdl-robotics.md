## Overview

**Creative Destruction Lab (CDL)** is a milestone-driven program for massively scalable, science- and tech-based startups. The **Robotics Stream** brings together accomplished tech entrepreneurs, angel investors, world-leading roboticists, and scientists to mentor early-stage hardware founders.

The hallmark of CDL is its **zero-equity model**: CDL takes **0% equity and 0% intellectual property**. Capital is invested directly by individual CDL Fellows and Venture Partners during the program’s intensive sessions.

---

## The Non-Dilutive Advantage

- **Equity Taken**: **0.0%**. CDL does not take founder shares, warrants, or advisory rights.
- **Capital**: Founders commonly raise between **$500k to $3M** directly from CDL mentor syndicates over the course of the 5 objective-setting sessions.
- **Milestone Accountability**: Every 8 weeks, mentors vote on which companies have hit their objectives. Those who succeed advance to the next round, building intense momentum toward closing funding.
