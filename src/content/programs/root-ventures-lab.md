## Overview

Root Ventures is a seed-stage venture fund led by engineers for engineers. Their **Embodied AI Lab Residency** offers a home for roboticists who want to build in a real machine shop rather than a sterile conference room.

With an on-site **OptiTrack motion capture cage**, dynamometer test stations, and Haas CNC machining centers, this program is designed to get your embodied AI models into real physical test loops within hours of pushing code.

---

## Investment Structure

- **Capital**: **$250,000** on an **Uncapped MFN SAFE** (Most Favored Nation). Root does not force a low valuation cap before you have proven your kinematics and market traction.
- **Hardware Allowance**: An extra **$15,000** dedicated grant specifically for purchasing motors, bearings, sensors, and PCBA spins.
- **Culture**: Zero mandatory pitches or guest lecture fluff. 100% focused on shop hours, bench testing, and technical milestones.
